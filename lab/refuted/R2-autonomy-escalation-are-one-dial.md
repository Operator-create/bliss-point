# R2 — "autonomy and escalation_explicitness are the same dial"

**Refuted 2026-08-19** by measurement, against an external recommendation to merge them.

An audit proposed collapsing seven dials to four, merging `acceptance_binding + verification_rigor`
and `autonomy + escalation_explicitness`.

**What killed it:** `bliss correlate` across the seven shipped profiles gives
`autonomy / escalation_explicitness` = **−0.632**. They move in *opposite* directions, which is what
you would expect if escalation is what you add when you take autonomy away, rather than a synonym
for it.

**What survived:** the other proposed merge, `acceptance_binding / verification_rigor` at +0.921, is
still a live candidate — filed under hypothesis/H3, not acted on, because all seven profiles have
one author.

**Also refuted in the same pass:** the expectation that `specificity` and `decomposition` track each
other. They are +0.604, among the weakest pairs. The tightest pair in the matrix,
`specificity / verification_rigor` at +0.978, was predicted by nobody.
