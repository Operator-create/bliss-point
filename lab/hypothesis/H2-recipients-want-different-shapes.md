# H2 — Different recipients want different shapes

**Status: OPEN. This is the project's central claim.** Test: the (C−F) interaction across the 2×2
of {small, large} × {two model families}.

Small execution models benefit from high specificity, decomposition and acceptance binding.
Frontier reasoning models benefit from high autonomy and low specificity. The *difference* is the
claim, not either half.

**Killed by:** both tiers preferring the same shape. That kills the thesis even if C beats F
everywhere.

**Confounded by:** vendor. If the preference tracks the family column rather than the size row,
the profiles are vendor lore, not a capability law — a real result, but a much smaller claim.
