## F8 was wrong, and the wrongness was the flattering kind

I read the new `/reasoning max` doctrine as saying one recipient now has two *shapes*, which would
have made it a variable the library cannot express — a constraint discovered in the wild,
corroborating the project's own thesis from a document written without reference to it.

Every one of the eight reasoning-mode lines assigns a **work class**, and the verb is `treat` in
every one. Line 597 is the closest thing to a shape instruction, *"Address the triad as you would
any specialist: with individually testable subtasks and /reasoning max at the receiver"*, and it is
unconditional: subtasks always, mode as well. Never "if elevated, then shape differently".

So the profile key is intact, `melchior` has one shape and two work classes, and the real defect is
a **coupling** in the dispatcher: who-to-send-to and what-mode-they-are-in are decided together and
checked against each other nowhere. Harness-side, under D0, not a Bliss Point feature.

**H10 refuted the same evening it was filed**, by the fourth of the four killers it named for
itself — the one it said was cheapest and should be checked first. Filed 23:40, dead 23:48, before
any code. [R4](../refuted/R4-receiver-mode-is-not-a-shape-variable.md). This is the second time this

## F9 was overstated in the way it was framed, not in what it measured

The objection put to the reviewer was that any eighth point moves a seven-point Pearson correlation,
so 0.176 is arithmetic dressed as a discovery. Tested twice, independently, with different synthetic
profiles — mine while waiting, its own during the run — and both say the same thing.

| eighth profile drawn as | median shift | at least as large as astra's 0.176 |
|---|---|---|
| uniform random | 0.133 | 20% |
| resampled from the existing seven | 0.091 | 1% |
| hand-written archetypes | 0.091 | 0 of 8 |
| its 13 candidates (8 midpoints, 5 corners) | 0.023 | 2 of 13, both corner-occupiers |

Ordinary against an arbitrary point; extreme against a plausible one. And the per-pair result is
sharper than either headline: **12 of its 13 candidates leave `decomposition / context_volume`
between +0.728 and +0.827**, and the one that collapses it to +0.183 is the one built to sit in
astra's corner. So the cause is corner occupancy, and the correct statement of D6's caution is that
the matrix is fitted to a hull and does not generalise outside it. F9 retitled and rewritten to say
that instead of leading with the number.
